# 📊 Global Market Performance Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard providing a comprehensive analysis of global market performance, sales trends, product profitability, and sales agent efficiency — enabling strategic decisions on market expansion and resource allocation.

---

## 🧭 Project Overview
This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights across markets, products, and agents.  
It is designed to answer key business questions:
- Which regions and categories are driving the most revenue and profit?
- Who are the top-performing sales agents?
- How can marketing and operations optimize underperforming markets?

---

## 🧩 Key Insights

### 🌍 Market Analysis
- **APAC and LATAM** lead growth with YoY increases above **50%**, while **EMEA and Canada** lag behind average growth.  
- Central and Southeast Asia show strong revenue momentum and expanding margins.

### 📦 Product Analysis
- **Phones** and **Copiers** contribute over **50% of total revenue**, with Phones maintaining a higher profit margin (~16–17%).  
- Sub-categories such as **Chairs** and **Bookcases** show lower profitability, especially in the EMEA region.

### 👩‍💼 Sales Agent Analysis
- **Anna Andreadi** and **Chuck Magee** are top performers in total revenue and YoY growth (>49%).  
- **Alejandro Ballentine** generates high sales volume but low margin (~2%), suggesting pricing or cost control issues.

### 💡 Recommendations
- Reallocate marketing and logistics budgets to **APAC** and **LATAM** to capture growth.  
- Conduct performance training for **Canada agents** to boost conversion rates.  
- Diversify product mix in **EMEA**, focusing on higher-margin items (Phones, Copiers).  
- Review cost structure for sub-categories with margins below **5%**.

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- Dynamic filters: Year, Market, Region, and Category.
- Drill-down analysis by **Product**, **Market**, and **Sales Agent**.
- Key KPIs tracked:

  - **Profit:** 1.47M (+52.3% YoY)
